About Me

I’m Khlood Osama — an IT student who loves turning ideas into real, working systems. What excites me most is not just writing code, but building complete solutions — from designing secure network infrastructures to integrating sensors, microcontrollers, and mobile applications into smart systems that actually solve problems.

I enjoy working at the intersection of Networking & Security, IoT & Embedded Systems, Mobile Applications (Flutter), and Smart Automation Solutions. I’ve designed enterprise-level network topologies using VLANs, OSPF, ACLs, NAT, and high-availability protocols. At the same time, I’ve built smart systems using ESP8266/ESP32, integrating gas sensors, lighting automation, and real-time monitoring through mobile apps

One of my proudest achievements was developing a commercial-ready Smart Home System, which ranked 4th at the 2nd International Conference on Technological Education (SCTE2025). That project wasn’t just a prototype — it was built with scalability, safety, and user experience in mind.
