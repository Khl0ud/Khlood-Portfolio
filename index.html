About Me

I'm Khlood Osama — an IT graduate who loves turning ideas into real, working systems. What excites me most is not just writing code, but building complete solutions — from designing secure network infrastructures to integrating sensors, microcontrollers, and mobile applications into smart systems that actually solve problems.

I enjoy working at the intersection of Networking & Security, IoT & Embedded Systems, Mobile Applications (Flutter), and Smart Automation Solutions. I've designed enterprise-level network topologies using VLANs, OSPF, ACLs, NAT, and high-availability protocols. At the same time, I've built smart systems using ESP8266/ESP32, integrating gas sensors, lighting automation, and real-time monitoring through mobile apps.

My graduation project, IoT-Based Smart Village for Green Energy Optimization, was defended on July 7, 2026 and awarded an Excellent Grade — a fully integrated ecosystem combining IoT, Edge Computing, Cloud Computing, and mobile applications across Smart Home, Irrigation, Parking, Traffic, Lighting, Waste Management, Surveillance, and Power Management subsystems.

One of my earlier proud achievements was developing a commercial-ready Smart Home System, which ranked 4th at the 2nd International Conference on Technological Education (SCTE2025).

I completed the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI) Cybersecurity track as a Vulnerability Analyst & Penetration Tester, conducting security tests aligned with Mastercard's framework, and worked as a Coding Instructor with the Digital Egypt Cubs & Marvels Initiatives (DECI & DEMI).
